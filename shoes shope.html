About Stride House.

Three generations on one street.

What began as a single repair bench in 1994 is now the neighbourhood’s favourite shoe store. We still know most customers by name.

We measure both feet.

Length, width and arch. Then we watch you walk, because no two feet match and no two shoes should either.

Built to be repaired, not replaced.

Resoling, restitching and polish on every pair bought here, at cost, for life.

Built from the ground up.

Every pair starts with the sole. Ours is cushioned for all-day walks and grips on wet roads.
