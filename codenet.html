About CodeNet

CodeNet ek jagah hai jahan developers apne code snippets, mini-projects aur ideas ko ek scrollable feed ke roop mein share karte hain — bilkul reels jaisa flow, lekin content video nahi, code hota hai.

Video-first social apps par coding content dhoondhna mushkil hota hai. CodeNet sirf code-first posts ke liye banaya gaya hai — quick to scroll, easy to copy, aur seedha kaam ka.
